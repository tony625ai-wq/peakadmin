## How to Get First Clients

1. **Your network first** — message 10 people who own or work in small businesses. Not a pitch, just: "Hey, I've just launched PeekAdmin Solutions — admin retainer service for small businesses. Know anyone who might need it?"

2. **Facebook local business groups** — search for "[your area] business owners" groups and post your launch post

3. **LinkedIn connections** — anyone you've worked with who now runs a business

4. **Local Facebook Marketplace** — post as a service listing

5. **Nextdoor (neighbourhood app)** — "Local Services" section is great for small business owners

---

## How to Teach Clients to Use the System

When you onboard a client, send them this:

> "Here's how we'll work together:
> 1. I'll set up a shared Google Drive folder for your documents
> 2. I'll create a PO tracking spreadsheet for your business
> 3. You forward any supplier emails to me and I'll handle them
> 4. Every Friday I'll send you a short update on what I've done
> 5. End of month: full report + any savings found"
