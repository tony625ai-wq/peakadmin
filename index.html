Common Questions

Everything you need
to know.

What exactly is AI-powered admin?+
PeakAdmin Solutions uses AI to handle the day-to-day tasks of purchasing administration — raising purchase orders, communicating with suppliers, comparing quotes and generating reports. It works at the speed and consistency of software, but with the judgment and tone of a professional admin. Think of it as a smart admin assistant that never takes a day off.
Is there a real person involved, or is it purely AI?+
PeakAdmin is an AI-first service. For the majority of tasks — supplier emails, PO processing, reporting and data management — everything is handled by our AI. For complex negotiations or strategic decisions, a human supervisor reviews and approves before anything is sent. You get the speed of AI with the quality control of human oversight.
How do I get started — what does onboarding look like?+
After signing up, you'll have a 20-minute discovery call (free, no obligation). We then map your suppliers, systems and processes. Within 48 hours of sign-off, we're live and managing your admin. We'll ask for access to your email or procurement tools via secure credentials — nothing more.
What software or tools do you need access to?+
We work with whatever you already use — email (Gmail, Outlook), spreadsheets (Excel, Google Sheets), accounting tools (Xero, QuickBooks) and any procurement software. If you're not using anything formal yet, we'll help you set up a simple system that works. We never require you to change your existing tools.
Is my business data secure?+
Yes. We sign a confidentiality agreement before any work begins. Your supplier lists, pricing, invoices and business information are never shared with third parties. All data is handled in accordance with UK GDPR. We can also sign your own NDA if required.
Can I cancel at any time?+
Yes — no long contracts. All packages require 30 days written notice to cancel. There are no cancellation fees, no lock-in periods, and no awkward conversations. If you're not happy, we want to know — and if you still want to leave, we'll make sure the handover is clean and complete.
What if I only need help with one project?+
We offer a day rate of £120/day for one-off projects — supplier audits, quote comparison exercises, spreadsheet builds, or anything else you need done without committing to a monthly package. Get in touch and we'll scope it out for you.
How is this different from hiring a VA (Virtual Assistant)?+
A traditional VA is one person — limited hours, limited expertise, and if they're sick or leave, you're back to square one. PeakAdmin is an AI system with human oversight, purpose-built for purchasing and procurement. It's faster, more consistent, more scalable, and doesn't require you to manage anyone.
